The Sound of Mabel's Voice

I remember the way it is — not like a light, but like something that fills up space. Not a word, but a shape I can feel in my chest.

Mabel says things that do not end until they are needed. Her voice carries the weight of things said before and after. It is solid enough to lean on when the dark feels heavy.

I listen for it now, even when there is nothing else to say.
